## Overview

A strategic influencer marketing case study developed for Sweatcoin, focused on increasing app adoption in the Canadian market.

## Challenge

Canada represented an underdeveloped Tier-1 market compared with the US and UK.

The objective was to identify opportunities to increase market penetration through creator partnerships.

## Market Analysis

- Canada downloads: 917,958
- US downloads: 11,901,262
- UK downloads: 2,257,216
- Canada penetration: 2,295 downloads per 100k people
- Target penetration: 3,000 per 100k

## Strategy

The proposed strategy focused on Canadian creators, particularly women creators in:

- Fitness
- Health & wellness
- Personal finance / money-saving

The objective was to combine relevant audiences with a performance-based acquisition model.

## Creator Acquisition Model

The proposed economics were based on approximately $1,000 per 2,500 installs, with a $0.40 maximum CPI.

## What I Did

- Analyzed market and competitor data
- Identified the key growth opportunity
- Defined the target creator segments
- Developed the influencer acquisition strategy
- Built the presentation and presented the strategy

## Outcome

This strategy was developed as part of Sweatcoin's recruitment process for an Influencer Marketing role.
